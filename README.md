# cloud-security-remote-work-risk-assessment
A cybersecurity risk assessment exploring threats related with cloud services and remote work, I have thought about practical examples and I've recommended security measures, according to what I am learning in my academic course.
The project explores how cloud services and remopte work can affect cybersecurity risks in modern organizations.
The examples I have here are based in a fictional company, and the exercise is created by my course Cybersecurity Studies – Sweden from Xedrix.

1. AI-genererad phishing
Cloud services:
Attackers can use AI to create realistic emails that trick users into sharing their login details for cloud services, such as Microsoft 365. If an account is hacked, the attacker may gain access to emails and files.

Remote Work:
Employees often work outside the office and communicate online. This can make it harder to know if a message really comes from a colleague or manager.

2. Deepfakes
Cloud services:
Cloud services can store audio, videos, and other information that may be used to create deepfakes. An attacker could pretend to be a manager and try to trick an employee into sharing sensitive information or approving a payment, for example

Remote Work:
During online meetings, a deepfake can be used to imitate a colleague or manager. It may be harder to verify someone's identity when communication happens through video calls or phone calls.

3.Weak Passwords and Account Takeover 
Cloud services:
Employees may use weak or reused passwords to access cloud platforms. 

Remote Work:
Employees may access company systems from different devices and locations. If they do not use strong passwords and MFA, the accounts may be at risk.
